About Me

Software Engineer • Fundamental Business Research • Lifelong Learner

Hello 👋

I'm Prem Kumar, a software engineer with over a decade of experience designing and building software systems.

Outside software development, I spend a significant amount of my free time studying businesses, industries and long-term wealth creation through fundamental analysis.

My research is based primarily on publicly available information such as annual reports, investor presentations, conference call transcripts, financial statements and industry reports.

Why This Website?

This website is my public research notebook.

Instead of chasing daily stock prices or market noise, I prefer understanding how businesses operate, how industries evolve and what creates sustainable competitive advantages over long periods of time.

Writing my research publicly helps me organize my thoughts and continuously improve my understanding.

Research Philosophy

📖 Read Before Investing

Annual reports, investor presentations and conference calls provide much deeper insights than short news articles.

🏭 Understand the Business

I focus on products, customers, supply chains, competitive positioning and industry dynamics before looking at valuation.

📈 Long-Term Thinking

Wealth is generally created by owning quality businesses over long periods rather than reacting to short-term market movements.

⚖ Risk Matters

Every company has risks. Understanding them is just as important as identifying growth opportunities.
