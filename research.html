Research

Understanding businesses, industries and long-term competitive advantages.

How I study a company

The objective isn't to predict where a stock will trade tomorrow. It is to understand the underlying business and determine what could make it more or less valuable over the next several years.

My research typically combines annual reports, investor presentations, conference calls, financial statements, exchange filings and industry research.

RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Six questions before valuation

01 — What does it do?

Products, customers, geography, revenue streams and business model.

02 — How big is the opportunity?

Industry size, structural growth, demand drivers and market share.

03 — Why can it win?

Technology, manufacturing capability, scale, customer relationships or other competitive advantages.

04 — Who runs it?

Management quality, capital allocation, governance and execution history.

05 — What do the numbers say?

Revenue, margins, cash generation, debt, ROCE, ROE and capital intensity.

06 — What can go wrong?

Competition, cyclicality, customer concentration, debt, regulation and execution risks.
