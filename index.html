THE IDEA

Think like a business owner.

Stock prices change every day. Businesses don't.

I use publicly available information to understand how companies actually make money, where their growth could come from, what could go wrong and whether their competitive position can survive over time.

Xlerator is my public research notebook — a place to document that process and continuously improve my understanding.

RESEARCH APPROACH

Start with the business.

Before thinking about valuation, I try to understand the underlying business and the industry in which it operates.

01 — Business

What does the company actually do? Who are its customers? Why do they buy from it?

02 — Industry

How large can the opportunity become? What drives demand? Who are the competitors?

03 — Moat

Does the company have technology, scale, relationships, switching costs or another durable advantage?

04 — Management

How does management allocate capital? Are promises followed by execution?

05 — Financials

Revenue growth, margins, cash flow, balance sheet and return on capital.

06 — Valuation

Finally — what expectations are already reflected in the market price?
